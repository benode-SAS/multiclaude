# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Discuss the code, not the person. Assume the other side is acting in good
faith until proven otherwise.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism about the work: what is wrong, why, and what would be better.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. A project has a direction, and "no" is
  not a judgement on the contributor.
- Respect the time of whoever reads you: a reproducible report and a focused pull request
  are worth more than volume.

## What is not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, publishing someone's private
information, and deliberate derailing of a discussion. This applies to issues, pull
requests, commit messages and any other space carrying the project's name.

## Reporting

Write to benjamin@benode.fr. Reports stay private. Depending on what happened, the
answer ranges from a warning to a permanent ban from the project's spaces — the
maintainers decide, and they will say what they decided.

This document is deliberately short. It exists so the rules are stated, not to cover
every case in advance.
